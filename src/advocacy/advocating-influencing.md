# Advocating & Influencing

A key RRCoP goal is to connect the expertise of the regulated research community with organizations that can highlight for policymakers the impact of federal regulations on the higher education research enterprise. Please see below for examples of relevant organizations whose advocacy RRCoP has helped to inform.
