# Join RRCoP

The primary way to join the Regulated Research Community of Practice is the **HigherEdCUI Slack community** — an informal community exchange with 1600+ members and daily on-topic conversations. When joining, use the name standard FIRST LAST (Institution).

- [Join the HigherEdCUI Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/higheredcui/shared_invite/zt-5f81k2he-76Prue4J_P8TunGAeySQfg)

Looking for more ways to participate — webinar teams, Ask the Assessor, advocacy, research security cohorts, or website curation? See [Get Involved](/about/get-involved/).

Questions? Contact us at [info@regulatedresearch.org](mailto:info@regulatedresearch.org).
