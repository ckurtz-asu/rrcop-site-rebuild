# Advocacy

Together we can amplify the impact of research subject to regulations.

As one of RRCoP's goals, is to advocate, influence, and negotiate for the voice of higher education institutions impacted by various regulations of their research. This involves responding to request for feedback, sharing small stories, and providing university leadership tailored information.
