## Bridging the Gap: Developing a Regulated Research Implementation Guide

August 13, 2025 | Strategic Partnership

Presented By: Will Drake and Tim Daniel, Indiana University

The Trusted CI Regulated Research Implementation Guide (TRRIG) is an effort to bridge a gap between two respected resources: the Trusted CI Framework Implementation Guide (FIG) and the Community Perspectives Guide. While the FIG is effective in structuring cybersecurity programs and Community Perspectives in capturing institutional values and challenges, neither are written specifically for regulated research environments.

TRRIG seeks to translate the strengths of both guides into a single, practical implementation roadmap tailored specifically for regulated research. It adopts the proven structure of the FIG and adapts it to meet the needs of institutions navigating complex compliance requirements. Our analysis revealed strong structural alignment between the Community Perspectives Pillars and the Trusted CI Framework Musts, giving us confidence that this adaptation retains the insights and integrity of both original documents.

## Trusted CI & RRCoP: The Next Five Years

December 11, 2024 | Strategic Partnership

Presented by:

- Sean Peisert, Trusted CI Director and PI, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
- Scott Russell, Trusted CI Deputy Director and Framework Lead, Indiana University
- Carolyn Ellis, Trusted CI CoPI and Regulated Research Lead, Arizona State University

Trusted CI, the NSF Cybersecurity Center of Excellence, has been a [resource partner](/about/whos-participating) since RRCoP's creation and has some big news about what The Next Five Years looks like. Trusted CI enables trustworthy NSF science by partnering with cyberinfrastructure (CI) operators to build and maintain effective cybersecurity programs, publishing resources that are valuable to the broader NSF community, and supporting the processes, tools, and knowledge to secure NSF research progress. As of October 1, 2024, Trusted CI has expanded to support regulated research by leveraging the established efforts of RRCoP. This webinar shares resources from the past, and what the services will look like moving forward.

## Indiana University's HIPAA Journey & Introduction to HITRUST

Presented By: Anurag Shankar, Indiana University; Michael Parisi, HITRUST

July 13, 2022 | Training Topic

HIPAA and Protected Health Information (PHI) have been a presence within healthcare and medical schools since 2005. In the years since, they have been leaking steadily into central IT and HPC centers as biomedical research computing needs have grown. This month Anurag Shankar from Indiana University talks about how IU implemented HIPAA for its central research cyberinfrastructure in 2007 and how its approach has evolved since then. HITRUST presents the various resources, tools and solutions available for organizations to leverage in managing risk and compliance, walking through how their programs allow organizations to "assess once, report many" as it relates to executing one validated assessment to provide assurances over compliance with multiple authoritative sources including HIPAA, NIST, CMMC, ISO, etc.
