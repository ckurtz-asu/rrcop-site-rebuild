## EDUCAUSE Policy Overview (most recent)

Presented by: Jarret Cummings, Senior Advisor for Policy and Government Relations, EDUCAUSE

September 11, 2024 | Strategic Partner

Research cybersecurity has remained an active space for federal agencies in 2024. As an RRCoP Strategic Partner, EDUCAUSE has worked with members that participate in both organizations to assess an array of developments and submit higher education responses. Join EDUCAUSE senior advisor for policy and government relations, Jarret Cummings, to review the major issues addressed to date as well as pending topics as we head into the latter part of the year.
