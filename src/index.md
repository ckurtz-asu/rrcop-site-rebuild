<section>
  <h2>Who is RRCoP?</h2>
  <p>We are research institutions that support research subject to compliance regulations,
    working together to grow the knowledge pool and produce efficiencies.</p>
  <p><a href="https://youtu.be/_kpsTUuUQ3g">Watch the four minute introduction (RRCoP Introduction)</a></p>

  <p>Our project deliverables include:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Hosting Monthly Webinars</li>
    <li><a href="/resources/">Sharing Resources</a></li>
    <li>Workshops</li>
  </ul>

  <p>We have 400+ institutions involved. See our
    <a href="/about/">partners and stakeholders</a>.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Key reference download</h2>
  <p>Following our Advanced System Security Plan Workshop, the community produced a partially
    completed SSP built from a new system diagram and 43 controls, designed to be a reference
    when working on your own SSP.</p>
  <p><a href="/resources/peer-practices/ssp/">Learn more &amp; download</a></p>
</section>
