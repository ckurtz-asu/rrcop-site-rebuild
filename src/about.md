# About RRCoP

We are research institutions that support research subject to compliance regulations
(CMMC, PHI, SOC 2, and more), working together to grow the knowledge pool and produce
efficiencies across the higher-education research security community.

## Get involved

Reach out to [info@regulatedresearch.org](mailto:info@regulatedresearch.org) to learn how
your institution can participate.

## Who's participating?

RRCoP includes 400+ institutions. A full partner/stakeholder map is planned for this
