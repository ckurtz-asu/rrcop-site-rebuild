# Tools & Templates

## Higher Education

### Recent
* [AI Assistant Development Resources for Regulated Research](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fo/uv95dqeadmh6wp745zpa0/ADtG1HY3lfb2FtK71vNBgAY?rlkey=ar0jchnqkcq23widwqossx750&st=w4queecf&dl=0) created by: Tommy Tunks, University of Michigan
  * These materials were developed as part of an ongoing effort to explore the responsible use of AI in research administration and research security contexts. The resource package includes assistant prompts, curated knowledge-base materials, evaluation question sets, and supporting tools used for testing, prompt refinement, and quality assurance. The materials are shared as examples of an iterative development process rather than a prescriptive solution. Institutions should adapt them to their own policies, risk tolerance, and governance frameworks.
* 2026 - [CMMC Level 2 Ver 2.13 Assessment Controls and Objectives](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bfEc1scItlMjMFp5rMf0xvs5DG5yzUwE/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=112810865999424666293&rtpof=true&sd=true) created by: Michael Wilson, UConn

## CISA - Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Agency
* [https://www.cisa.gov/free-cybersecurity-services-and-tools](https://www.cisa.gov/free-cybersecurity-services-and-tools) includes ~100 collected tools designed to:
  1. Reducing the Likelihood of a Damaging Cyber Incident
  2. Take Steps to Quickly Detect a Potential Intrusion
  3. Ensure That The Organization is Prepared to Respond if an Intrusion Occurs
  4. Maximize the Organization's Resilience to a Destructive Cyber Incident
