# Poll Results & Community Collected Data

As part of our ongoing effort to surface community practices, we typically start each RRCoP webinar with a quick poll. [Join Us](/monthly-webinars), on the 2nd Wednesday of the month at 11 am PT / 2 pm ET. These snapshot questions help us understand where institutions stand, highlight shared challenges, and inform national planning efforts. The results are now available on our website for broader use and reflection.

To reference this collection of institutional polling results in presentations, publications, or grant proposals, please use the following citation:

> Regulated Research Community of Practice (RRCoP). (2025). Institutional Polling Results Archive. Retrieved from https://www.regulatedresearch.org/resources/polls
