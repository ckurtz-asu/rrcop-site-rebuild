## A Community of Communities — Strategic Fit

RRCoP spans multiple stakeholder domains and bridges between other existing communities.

RRCoP brings the most value by overlapping with many other communities that share parts of the complete workflow of regulated research.

Help make this community of communities stronger! Are there other communities of alignment? Send suggestions to [info@regulatedresearch.org](mailto:info@regulatedresearch.org).
